Wispr Max · built on Wispr Flow

A little AI buddy on your desktop that teaches you how to do the work.

Max floats over every app. Ask it anything out loud and it shows you, step by step, pointing at the exact button. It talks first, roasts you with memes and slang, and gets to know you.

  • Teaches, doesn't just answer. "How do I edit my submission?" Max flies to the button, says what to click, and waits for you.
  • Speaks first. It says hi, notices a rejection email or a 3am error, and reacts before you ask.
  • Memes, slang, Bollywood sounds. Roast it and it roasts back. Swear at it and you get "ye angrezi gaali apne paas rakho".
  • Yours. Say "Max, remember I'm vegetarian" and every answer fits you. It keeps the conversation going until Esc, understands Hindi and Hinglish, and writes in your style.
  • Shows the whole path. A step checklist ("2/4"), it flies to each button, and when a website is faster it just opens it for you.
