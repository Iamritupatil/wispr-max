Why I built it

Every time I got stuck on a website (where's the edit button, how do I submit this), I left my flow: screenshot, open ChatGPT, explain the whole page, read a wall of steps, switch back. On the free plan I'd even hit the upload limit.

So I built Max: a little pixel ghost that lives on top of every app. You ask out loud, and it shows you, right on your screen. It talks first, it reacts with meme sounds and GIFs, it understands Hindi and Hinglish, and if your question is too obvious, it roasts you.

Funny thing: I was building something completely different for this hackathon. I woke up one morning, decided I wasn't going to build that, scrapped it, and built Max from scratch.

How it works

I first used AssemblyAI for speech-to-text, but it kept mishearing my accent. Wispr Flow understood me, so every request to Max goes through Flow first: Flow is the ears, Max adds the brain, a mouth and hands.

Three ways to use Max

No install

In your browser

  1. Open the dashboard.
  2. Pick a sample app (or share your own tab).
  3. Ask "how do I…?". Max circles the button, with a roast, a GIF and a meme sound.
Open dashboard

Any website

Chrome extension

  1. Download it and Extract All.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the wispr-max-extension folder (the one with manifest.json inside).
  3. On any site, click the ghost in your toolbar (or press Alt+M) and ask with Wispr Flow. Max points at the real button on the page.
Download extension

Every app

Desktop app

  1. Download and install it.
  2. Tap Alt twice and talk: Max starts Wispr Flow for you. Tap Alt twice again when you're done.
  3. Max flies onto the button in any app, writes where you type, and hands big tasks to Claude Code. Esc stops everything.
Download

Questions

What does Wispr Flow do here?

Flow is Max's ears. You talk with your normal Flow key; Flow turns it into text and Max takes it from there. Flow understood my accent far better than the speech-to-text I tried first.

Does Max watch my screen all the time?

No. Only when you ask: one screenshot goes to Claude with that question, and nothing is stored.

Is it free?

Yes. Max runs on the Wispr Max server, so there's no API key to set up.

Does it understand Hindi?

Yes: Hindi and Hinglish, in Roman letters or Devanagari. Max answers in English, with Bollywood meme sounds.

How do I stop it?

Press Esc. Voice, memes, the walkthrough: everything stops.

Will it really roast me?

If your question is obvious, yes. Lovingly. Ask it "Am I the best coder alive?" and find out.
